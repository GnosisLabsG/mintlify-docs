## Overview

Share links provide the easiest way to give people access to your AI agent. No setup required - just copy and share a URL.

<Info>
  Perfect for demos, customer support, team collaboration, or whenever you need quick access to your agent.
</Info>

## Getting Your Share Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Share">
    Go to **Integration** → **Share** in your Mind-e dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy Your Link">
    Click the **Copy** button next to your unique share link
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share Anywhere">
    Send the link via email, messaging apps, or social media
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your share link looks like this:
```
https://mind-e.co/embed/chat.php?id=b29c2c13-ce50-4da9-84f9-dcd715d9da5a
```

<Note>
  Anyone with this link can interact with your AI agent. All conversations will be recorded in your chat history.
</Note>

## Customizing Your Experience

### URL Parameters

Add parameters to customize the chat experience:

| Parameter | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| `theme=dark` | Use dark theme | `&theme=dark` |
| `welcome=Hello!` | Custom welcome message | `&welcome=Hello%20there!` |
| `fullscreen=true` | Remove branding | `&fullscreen=true` |

**Example with parameters:**
```
https://mind-e.co/embed/chat.php?id=your-id&theme=dark&welcome=How%20can%20I%20help?
```

## Security Considerations

<Warning>
  Share links are public by default. Anyone with the link can access your agent.
</Warning>

### Access Control Options

- **Password Protection** - Require a password to access
- **Time Restrictions** - Limit availability to business hours  
- **Domain Restrictions** - Only allow access from specific websites
- **Usage Limits** - Set maximum number of conversations

Configure these in **Settings** → **Security** in your dashboard.

## Tracking Performance

Monitor how your share links perform:

- **Total Clicks** - Number of people who accessed your link
- **Conversations Started** - How many actually chatted with your agent
- **Popular Times** - When users are most active
- **Referrer Sources** - Where your traffic comes from

View analytics in **Analytics** → **Share Links** in your dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Link not working" icon="alert-triangle">
    - Check that your agent is active and published
    - Verify the URL is complete and correct
    - Test in incognito mode to rule out browser issues
    - Ensure no access restrictions are blocking users
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agent not responding" icon="message-x">
    - Verify agent has knowledge sources configured
    - Check agent status in dashboard
    - Test the agent in Playground first
    - Look for error messages in the chat interface
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Low engagement" icon="trending-down">
    - Improve your sharing message with clear context
    - Test different platforms and messaging
    - Make the value proposition clearer
    - Consider adding QR codes for mobile users
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
